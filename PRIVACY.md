# Privacy Policy for Freezer

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Freezer is developed by Ruifan Wang. Freezer does not collect or transmit your inventory, grocery list, photos, or activity to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server.

## Records stay on your device

Food names, dates, portions, private notes, storage areas, grocery records, preferences and consumption history are stored locally. Freezer does not provide cloud synchronization. Your device-level backup settings may include app data in Apple or computer backups; those copies are controlled by you and your operating system.

## Camera and photo library

Camera access is optional and used only when you choose Scan label. You may instead select an image through Apple's photo picker. Apple Vision recognizes text on your device. Freezer does not upload photos, keep scanned images in inventory, or include scanned photos in its exported backups. Images used for a scan are temporary. Confirming a result saves the name you choose.

## Reminders

Optional reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. No push-notification server receives your food records. Notification text may show food information on your device according to your notification and lock-screen settings.

## Backups and sharing you choose

When you export a backup, it includes your records and private notes. You choose its destination in Files. A selected cloud Files provider may store that backup under its own terms. Keep backups private.

When you share a card, document or text, Freezer prepares selected content locally and passes it to the app or destination you choose through Apple's share sheet. Private food notes are excluded from shares; selected shopping quantity notes are included. Share files are temporary and cleaned up after use. The receiving service handles the shared content under its own privacy practices. Freezer does not track recipients or confirm delivery.

## External links and support

Opening food-guidance or support links takes you to third-party websites. Those sites may process information under their own policies. If you contact us through GitHub, we receive the information you choose to post. GitHub issues are public: do not include private records, photos or backups. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Deleting your data

You can remove records in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Separately exported backups, shared copies and device backups are not removed by deleting app records; manage or delete those copies at their destinations.

## Children's privacy

Freezer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. The same local-storage behavior applies to all users.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date. For privacy questions or help, use the [support page](README.md).
