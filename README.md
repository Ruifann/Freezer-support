# Freezer — Support

Freezer helps you remember what is in your freezer and fridge, where you put it, and when you stored it. Organize shelves, drawers and door bins; record portions; keep a grocery list; scan one food label at a time; and share selected lists or weekly summaries.

## Contact and help

For help, bug reports or feature requests, [open a support issue](https://github.com/Ruifann/Freezer-support/issues/new) or [view existing issues](https://github.com/Ruifann/Freezer-support/issues). A GitHub account is required to post. This is the developer's monitored support channel.

Include your iOS version, app version, what you expected, and steps to reproduce the problem. Issues are public: do not attach private inventory, photos, backup files, contact details, or other sensitive information.

## Common questions

### Does Freezer need an account or internet connection?
No account is needed. Inventory, grocery suggestions and label recognition work on your device. External guidance links and the app you choose for sharing may need internet access.

### How do I protect my records?
In Settings, use Backup and restore to export a backup to Files. Keep a copy outside the app. Restore replaces the current inventory after confirmation; Freezer first creates a recovery copy. Deleting the app removes its local records. Independently exported backups remain wherever you saved them.

### Does label scanning identify everything in a fridge?
No. Scan one visible label, review the recognized text, and confirm or edit the food name. Recognition can make mistakes. It does not determine freshness, quantities, container contents, or storage dates.

### Are suggested use dates food-safety guarantees?
No. Dates are planning guidance based on recorded information. They do not measure temperature or freshness. Follow food labels and relevant food-safety guidance.

### Do shares create a shared account?
No. Sharing exports a snapshot through Apple's share sheet. Recipients cannot edit your inventory. Private food notes are excluded; grocery quantity notes are included when the corresponding entry is selected.

### Why didn't a reminder arrive?
Check notification permission in iOS Settings, the app's reminder settings, and Focus settings. iOS controls delivery. You can retry scheduling from the app when it reports a problem.
